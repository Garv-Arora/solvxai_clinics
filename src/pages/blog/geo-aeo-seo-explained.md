**The short version:** SEO gets your pages read. AEO makes a page easy to quote. GEO gets other sites to say what your pages say. You need all three, in that order.

## The three, side by side

| | What it is | Where the work happens | You know it worked when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEO** | Search engine optimisation | Your site and your Google profiles | Your pages are crawled, indexed and found |
| **AEO** | Answer engine optimisation | Single pages on your site | An AI quotes your page |
| **GEO** | Generative engine optimisation | Your site and everyone else's | An AI names your practice |

## SEO is the entry ticket

An AI answer is built from pages a crawler could read. If your site blocks the crawler, or needs JavaScript to show its text, nothing else matters.

Google says the same fundamentals apply to its AI features, and that tricks sold as AI levers, such as llms.txt or special schema, are not needed. ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide))

## AEO is one question, one page

The page an AI quotes has the question as its title, the answer in the first two sentences, numbers with sources, and a date. Pages that AI assistants cite are 25.7% fresher than pages in classic results. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/))

## GEO is what others say

This is the part most practices skip. Across 75,000 brands, mentions on other sites tracked with ChatGPT visibility at 0.664. The number of pages on a brand's own site: 0.194. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/))

And 43.8% of the pages ChatGPT cites are "best of" lists. ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/))

## Which first

1. Check the crawlers can read your site. An afternoon.
2. Write the answer pages for your five most valuable procedures.
3. Then spend most of your time off your site: directories, lists, reviews, local press.
